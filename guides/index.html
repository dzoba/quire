Reading files in Chrome, step by step

Short how-tos for EPUB books, PDFs and Markdown in Chrome and on a Chromebook. Each one starts with what Chrome can already do on its own, then shows where an extension helps and what else is worth trying.

  • How to open EPUB files in ChromeChrome downloads .epub files instead of opening them. How to read them in a tab.
  • How to open EPUB books on a ChromebookWhat ChromeOS does on its own, and four ways to read EPUB books.
  • How to fill a PDF form in Chrome without AdobeChrome’s built-in viewer fills many forms. When an extension helps, and when you still need Adobe.
  • How to highlight, annotate and sign a PDF in ChromeWhat Chrome’s viewer can already do, and what Quire PDF adds.
  • How to view Markdown (.md) files in ChromeChrome shows Markdown as plain text. Ways to see it formatted.
  • How to edit a PDF on a ChromebookThe built-in Gallery app adds text, highlights and drawings. What it can’t do, and what Quire PDF adds.
  • How to fill in and sign a PDF on a ChromebookFill in a form and place a signature with Gallery, and what a drawn signature is.
  • How to open Markdown (.md) files on a ChromebookGoogle Drive’s new Markdown preview, a Chrome extension, or a code editor under Linux.
